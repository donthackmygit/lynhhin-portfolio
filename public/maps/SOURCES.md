# World map

`world-map.svg` is a simplified, equirectangular land silhouette derived from
Natural Earth's 1:110m physical land dataset. Antarctica is omitted for framing.
The SVG has no national boundaries, scripts, external resources, or dependencies.

- Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
- License: public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- Regenerate: `node scripts/generate-world-map.mjs`
